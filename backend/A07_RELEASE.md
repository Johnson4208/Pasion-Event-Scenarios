# A07 intrinsic valuation and sensitivity

Builds on existing F04/A06 eligible model service. FCFF enterprise cash flows and stable terminal cash are discounted at WACC using target-date ACT/365 cash dates; equity subtracts net debt/lease/restricted-cash/NCI bridge and adds nonoperating assets before eligible diluted shares. FCFE discounts common-shareholder cash at ke. Rate/growth/margin sensitivities keep method low/base/high without forced consensus. Terminal stability is an explicit reviewed assumption; WACC<=g, nonpositive terminal cash and invalid share count withhold values.

P/E requires positive eligible annual EPS and reviewed normalized multiple. DDM/Gordon require recurring dividend policy and explicit next payment; ke must exceed growth. Bank/insurer sector policy cannot inherit industrial FCFF/FCFE. Asset-based method retains explicit adjusted equity/share assumptions. Method estimates now identify evidence_family, capital inputs and equity bridge components in the existing model details. FCFF/FCFE and DDM/Gordon are acknowledged correlated cross-checks, never votes for a composite intrinsic truth. known_at maximum uses actual offset-aware instants.

New finance/valuation.py gates method-specific valuation gaps against the reviewed F07 quote registry. Quote must be fresh, within its reviewed session, match today's valuation date in the valuation timezone and match estimate currency. Historical valuations require a future point-in-time quote adapter and stay unavailable. Invalid/corrupt quote context withholds only the gap, not the valuation. Formula is eligible per-share method value/current price minus one. No average estimate or stale/sample price is used. Fresh gaps expose source, as_of and instrument version. UI retains existing model cards/plot/details and displays separate eligible method gaps.

Required validation: existing valuation tests cover eligibility, periods, shares, perpetuity, sectors and immutable owner/scenario assumptions. New gap fixtures cover fresh 120/100-1=0.2, stale quote, historical date and currency mismatch. No live market quote or company fair value was certified by test fixtures. Production source/identity/storage review remains required.
