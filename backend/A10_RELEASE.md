# A10 trade outcomes and bounded planning

Shared feasibility remains the only sizing adapter for member Trade Planner and admin shadow Distribution. Optional reviewed instrument liquidity_max_quantity caps quantity at the floor-to-lot amount. When absent, liquidity is explicitly NOT_ASSESSED_DRAFT_ONLY; no market capacity is fabricated. BUY quantity respects cash after reserve/entry costs; SELL reduction respects own sellable holdings. Shorting remains withheld without future borrow/margin support. Cross-currency accounts still require verified FX support rather than an assumed conversion.

Default fixed-fractional sizing retains cost-adjusted stop risk. Optional risk_basis GAP_STRESS uses explicit gap_slippage_pct in loss-per-share sizing; stop prices are never represented as guaranteed maximum loss. Net reward/risk now uses target net PnL divided by absolute stop net PnL. Existing hypothetical target/stop/unresolved/gap scenarios, account/instrument versions, expiry and canonical DecisionEligibility remain. liquidity cap is admitted with the instrument review and quote lifetime, not an independent live tape estimate. Kelly stays withheld: return p_up, event judgments and descriptive outcome counts cannot supply validated binary payoff edge.

market/outcomes.py implements retrospective fixed-level first-touch scenarios. Enter next completed session open, reject next-open entry outside predeclared stop/target, exit gap stops at open, same-bar collisions stop first, favorable target gaps conservatively at target, unresolved at horizon close. Explicit quantity/fees/slippage apply to all paths. Original signal must be complete; unmatured/incomplete paths are excluded and reported separately. Summary reports wins/losses/unresolved, conditional target-hit among resolved vs unconditional among matured, and all-path net PnL. These are descriptive, potentially overlapping scenarios, not independent calibrated probabilities or Kelly inputs.

Authenticated POST /api/trades/outcomes consumes reviewed technical snapshots, ticker, unique origins (1–200), horizon, fixed stop/target, direction, quantity, fee_rate, fixed_fee and slippage_bps. Costs are explicit research assumptions; no historical broker fee or order-fill certification. Returned source hash/as-of and path indices/timestamps identify evidence. It does not mutate accounts, reserves or orders. No new historical-outcome UI or automatic stop-selection optimizer was added; native local drafts/export remain intact. Research responses have run IDs but no persistent backtest archive in this thin release.

Acceptance tests cover cash1000/entry101/lot10=>0, source-specific lot liquidity cap, lower quantities under gap stress, same-bar stop-first, gap-at-open, unresolved close, insufficient maturity, conditional40/60 vs unconditional40/100 and existing BUY/SELL constraints. Production quote/calendar/cost and independent edge validation remain required before executable use.
